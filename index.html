AI project · 2026

Intelligent Traffic Safety Monitoring & Accident Detection System

Problem
Road-safety monitoring needs both detection of violations as they happen and an estimate of where risk is highest.
What it does
Detects traffic violations such as missing helmets and accidents from video or a webcam, crops plate and subject evidence, and predicts accident and violation risk from location, density, weather and hour of day. SHAP and LIME explain each prediction.
Approach
YOLOv8 for detection, a CNN and XGBoost for classification and risk, Tesseract OCR for plates, and generative AI APIs, all shown in an interactive dashboard.
  • Python
  • YOLOv8
  • CNN
  • XGBoost
  • OpenCV
  • Tesseract OCR
  • SHAP
  • LIME
